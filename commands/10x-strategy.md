name: 10x-strategy
description: Turn advertiser and buyer evidence into prioritized creative opportunities and a next test.
---

Apply [10X Creative Strategist](../skills/10x-creative-strategist/SKILL.md) to the current request and project context. Connect each recommended opportunity to a buying situation, message, proof, execution and test hypothesis. Rank distinct directions by relevance, evidence, clarity, brand fit and feasibility. Use existing evidence first; invoke the active advertiser-intelligence workflow only for research within scope. Preserve unknowns and the user's choice. Do not launch production merely because its tools are available.
